Wald’s Protocol Validation Methodology

In satellite super-resolution without concurrent sub-meter airborne reference, Wald’s Protocol is the remote sensing standard: the 2.5m super-resolved image is filtered with the sensor point-spread function (area-averaging degradation model) and quantitatively compared against the original 10m Sentinel-2 observation. High PSNR, SSIM, and near-zero SAM mathematically prove zero spectral distortion.

About SentinelSR

A complete deep learning solution for Sentinel-2 satellite super-resolution designed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH), enhancing 10m multispectral imagery to 2.5m with uncertainty estimation and physical spectral conservation.

🧠

LDSR-S2 Latent Diffusion

Trained on Sentinel-2 L2A BOA reflectance. Uses latent diffusion denoiser with DDIM sampling to synthesize sub-pixel high-frequency structures without spectral bias.

🎯

Uncertainty Quantification

Evaluates epistemic variance across stochastic diffusion sampling passes to identify areas of potential hallucination and provide confidence guarantees.

📊

Wald’s Protocol Assessment

Calculates PSNR, SSIM, SAM, and ERGAS against sensor physical degradation models, ensuring rigorous remote sensing accuracy standards.

🌱

Downstream Impact

Delivers 2.5m precision agriculture (NDVI), disaster and flood damage mapping (NDWI), and analysis-ready georeferenced GeoTIFF exports.
