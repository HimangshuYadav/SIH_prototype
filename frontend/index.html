SentinelSR Architecture

Sentinel-2 multispectral super-resolution engine enhancing 10m imagery to 2.5m resolution with physical radiometric preservation and GIS export compatibility.

Real-Data ESRGAN (Ours)

Trained from scratch on over 7,700 real paired satellite patches from Sen2Venµs (5m) and WorldStrat (2.5m). Achieves 39.19 dB PSNR with 0.33s latency.

LDSR-S2 Baseline

Latent diffusion baseline model evaluated under iterative DDIM sampling steps for comparison.

Wald Synthesis Protocol

Computes PSNR, SSIM, SAM, and ERGAS metrics against the sensor point spread function model.

GIS Products

Generates 2.5m NDVI, NDWI, and projected GeoTIFF rasters with intact spatial transforms.
